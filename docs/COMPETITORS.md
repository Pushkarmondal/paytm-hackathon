# Competitor review (bounded)

**Purpose.** `docs/PRD.md` §2 treats differentiation as a hypothesis: that existing explainers, aggregators and insurer tools explain the room-rent rule in general but do not apply a user's own wording to the user's own estimate, item by item, with the clause shown and the unknowns listed. This review checks that hypothesis within the limits below.

**Method.** Two web searches on Saturday 24 October 2026, read from search-result snippets only. No product was used hands-on, no app was installed, and no page beyond the snippet was verified. That is a weak method; it can show what is visible, not what does not exist.

**What the search surfaced.**

| Source | What it appears to offer | Applies your wording to your bill? | Clause shown? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Outlook Money, "Understanding proportionate deductions" | explainer article with a general example | no | no |
| Mediassist blog, "Impact of room rent limit on health insurance claims" | explainer with a worked percentage example; notes medicines are typically excluded | no | no |
| Turtlemint, proportionate deduction article | explainer with a worked example (₹1.5 lakh bill, ₹5,000 limit, ₹6,000 room) | no | no |
| freefincal, "Claiming medical expenses from two policies" | explainer; applies the limit ratio to the whole bill in its example | no | no |
| ManipalCigna "answers" page on proportionate deduction | insurer FAQ | no | no |
| Reliance General knowledge centre, "IRDAI redefining sub-limit rules" | insurer article on the regulatory draft | no | no |
| Health Assist TPA app (App Store listing) | e-card, claim status, coverage lookup, network hospitals | no | no |
| ICICI Lombard IL TakeCare app (2024-era mention) | claim intimation from the app | no | no |
| Jio Insurance Brokers mediclaim page | product page | no | no |

**Finding.** Within this bounded search, no tool was found that takes a specific policy wording and a specific hospital estimate, classifies the bill's lines by that wording's own definition, computes the deductions item by item, shows the clause beside each number and states what it could not determine. Explainers apply one ratio to the whole bill in their examples; the apps found handle claim status and intimation.

**What this does and does not support.** It supports saying on the USP slide: "In a bounded review of nine public sources we found no tool that does this." It does not support "nobody does this", and the deck must not say that. Insurer and TPA portals may have internal estimators that a search does not surface; hands-on checks of the major aggregators' apps are the next step, scheduled for the week of 27 October if the team is shortlisted.
