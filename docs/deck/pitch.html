Problem statement

The policy's cost shows up at the hospital

Room-rent limits, proportionate deductions, sub-limits and exclusions surface at admission, not at purchase. The room-rent clause is the sharpest: pick a room above the eligible rate and many policies pay the surgeon, anaesthetist, nursing and operation-theatre charges only in the proportion eligible ÷ actual rate.

Proof, not promises

A real wording, quoted verbatim, with the conflict it contains

Insurer-issued · IRDAI-hosted · partially encoded

Arogya Sanjeevani Policy, The New India Assurance Co. Ltd.

UIN NIAHLIP20165V011920 · downloaded from irdai.gov.in on 24 October 2026 · 31 pages · ten clauses encoded with page numbers · SHA-256 4e261c78…f542d44 shown on screen

What the engine applies

Room 2% of SI up to ₹5,000 a day; ICU 5% up to ₹10,000; the proportion of all other expenses except medicines when either rate is exceeded; a 5% co-payment.

What it refuses to guess

This 2019-20 wording reduces implants and diagnostics; IRDAI's June 2020 norms later barred that. The engine shows those items as a range and says "requires insurer confirmation".

The ICU surprise

A ₹15,000 ICU day alone triggers the proportion under this wording, even in a twin-sharing room. When room and ICU exceed in different ratios, the engine gives bounds, not a number.

Measured

21 of 21 hand-computed amounts exact; 3 of 3 refusal cases right; every trace validated against the verbatim clause text.

Partial encoding, labelled so everywhere. The schedule used with it is synthetic; real schedules are personal and never bundled.

What ships today

Four tools around one engine

Room ladder

Every room on one view with the deductions at each; the highest room within the limit is marked. One click plans it.

Question sheet

Every open uncertainty becomes a question for the hospital or the insurer, tied to its clause and the rupees at stake. Copy, print or save.

Ruleset builder

A model proposes rules from a public wording with quotes; the engine verifies each quote verbatim; a person confirms. Cached proposals here; live adapter off.

Why this changed, and the evidence pack

Each change names its clause, the inputs that differ and both formulas. The whole result exports as a Markdown or JSON evidence pack with every trace.

Technology / tech stack

Deterministic engine, AI only with human review

Frontend

TypeScript, strict. Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, Tailwind 4. Six screens on a tested screen model; no business logic in components.

Rules engine

Pure TypeScript, no I/O, no model. Integer paise, exact ratios, one half-up rounding. A trace on every amount: rule, inputs, clause, formula, output, open assumptions.

Two wordings

A synthetic demo policy, and Arogya Sanjeevani (New India, from irdai.gov.in): ten clauses quoted verbatim, page-cited, SHA-256 shown. Partially encoded and labelled so.

AI, in its place

The Ruleset builder: a model proposes rules with quotes; the engine verifies each quote verbatim; a person confirms. Cached in this build; the live adapter is off. Never in the calculation path.

Quality

Vitest, 180 tests, written first. GitHub Actions on every PR. Evaluation harness: six measures over two wordings, results committed and checked for staleness.

Runs anywhere

One command, offline on localhost; fonts bundled; Vercel-ready. Nothing the user enters leaves the browser. No key, no account, no analytics, no database.

Data flow, all in the browser

fixture or upload (JSON, CSV) → ingestion: Zod schema + verbatim evidence check → screen model → rules engine: classify → limits → proportion → co-payment → traces → six screens, question sheet, evidence pack

AI seam, off by default: public clause text → model proposals → verbatim quote check → human confirmation → ruleset. No schedule, bill or name ever leaves the browser.

What we claim

Claim Sach turns a policy wording into a verifiable hospital-bill simulation: change the room, inspect every modelled deduction, and see what the documents cannot determine.

What we do not claim

Not the first to read clauses; not a payout guarantee; no accuracy figure beyond the measured ones. A bounded review of nine public sources found no tool doing this; we claim we looked, not that nobody does.

Business model

Where it fits Paytm, and what must be tested

Verified facts
  • Paytm Insurance Broking Pvt Ltd is a Direct (Life & General) broker: IRDAI Reg. No. 700, renewed 9 Feb 2026, valid to 16 Feb 2029.
  • It distributes car, two-wheeler, health, life and term policies from 18+ insurers, including Niva Bupa and Aditya Birla Health. It does not underwrite.
  • It already offers claim assistance by phone. Arogya Sanjeevani is IRDAI's standard product on one wording; one verified encoding serves every insurer that sells it.
  • FY25 turnover ₹37.1 crore, loss ₹13.2 crore (Form AOC-1), inside a ₹2,593 crore financial-services line in FY26. Insurance is a small line for Paytm today; clarity is a wedge worth testing.
