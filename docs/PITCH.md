# Claim Sach: pitch outline

Seven sections, as the devSemble listing requires. Facts only where marked verified in `docs/PRD.md`; everything else is a hypothesis and says so.

1. **Title slide.** Claim Sach: Coverage Twin. Track 2, AI-Powered Financial Journeys. "Know what your policy might leave you paying before admission."
2. **Problem statement.** People learn what their policy leaves them paying at the hospital. The room-rent clause is the sharpest example: a ₹2,500-a-day room gap that cuts the surgeon's fee by a third, under a definition only the policy wording can settle. General explainers stop at the concept; the gap we are testing is applying your own wording to your own bill.
3. **Proposed solution.** Policy documents → structured rules → scenario simulation → itemised potential deductions → source clause → explicit uncertainty. Screenshot: the Coverage Twin with "Change your room. See the difference." and the clause panel open.
4. **Technology / tech stack.** TypeScript, Next.js 16, Tailwind. A pure rules engine with integer-paise arithmetic and a trace on every amount. Ingestion adapters separate from the engine; uploaded rulesets refused if they misquote a clause. No database. No LLM in the calculation path; an adapter seam for candidate-clause extraction with human confirmation. Vitest, GitHub Actions. Runs offline.
5. **USP.** Item-level, clause-cited, uncertainty-marked, interactive. Unknown is never zero. Every number is clickable. Stated as what the product does; the bounded competitor review in `docs/COMPETITORS.md` found no tool doing this, and the slide says exactly that much.
6. **Impact and benefits.** For the policyholder: the room decision made with the deductions in view, before admission. For a broker's claim-assistance agent: the clause on screen during the call. Figures on this slide are potential deductions covered by the simulation, never total out-of-pocket cost. The evaluation results, with dataset sizes, as of the deck date.
7. **Business model.** Verified: Paytm Insurance Broking is a Direct (Life & General) broker (IRDAI Reg. No. 700, renewed 9 February 2026, valid to 16 February 2029), distributes health policies from 18+ insurers, does not underwrite, offers claim assistance by phone; FY25 turnover ₹37.1 crore with a ₹13.2 crore loss, inside a ₹2,593 crore financial-services line in FY26. Insurance is a small line for Paytm today. Hypotheses with tests: fewer claim-time support contacts (pilot comparison); more eligible-room choices (room-change rate, then claims data); insurer co-funding as a distribution-quality signal (three conversations, one signed pilot); a lift on the policy page (A/B). Bima Sugam only in the future tense.
